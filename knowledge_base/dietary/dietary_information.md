# Dietary & Milk Information Knowledge Base

---

MILK OPTIONS:
1. Whole Milk (Standard Dairy) - Used in Cappuccino, Latte, Mocha, Cold Coffee, Iced Latte, Caramel Macchiato, Chocolate Frappe, Masala Chai.
2. Oat Milk (Non-Dairy Plant Milk) - Used in Iced Vanilla Latte (Oat Milk).

NON-DAIRY & LACTOSE-INTOLERANT OPTIONS:
- Iced Vanilla Latte (Oat Milk) - Uses Oat Milk (contains Oats allergen, zero dairy milk).
- Espresso - No milk (Milk: None).
- Americano / Iced Americano - No milk (Milk: None).
- Green Tea / Peach Iced Tea - No milk (Milk: None).

ALLERGEN DECLARATIONS:
- Dairy/Milk Allergen: Present in Cappuccino, Latte, Mocha, Cold Coffee, Iced Latte, Caramel Macchiato, Chocolate Frappe, Masala Chai.
- Oats Allergen: Present in Iced Vanilla Latte (Oat Milk).
- No Known Allergens: Espresso, Americano, Iced Americano, Green Tea, Peach Iced Tea.

STRICT DIETARY SAFETY RULES:
- Only claim non-dairy if the product explicitly uses non-dairy milk (like Oat Milk) or uses no milk (Milk: None) and lists no dairy allergens.
- Do NOT invent nutritional claims, calorie counts, protein counts, or gluten-free/vegan claims unless explicitly stated in this knowledge base.
