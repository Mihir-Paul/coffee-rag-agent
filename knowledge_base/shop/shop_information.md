# Coffee Shop Information & Policies Knowledge Base

---

SHOP DETAILS:
- Shop Name: Coffee Shop RAG
- Currency: Indian Rupee (INR / ₹)
- Operating Hours: 7:00 AM - 10:00 PM (Monday - Sunday)
- Payment Methods: UPI, Cash, Credit/Debit Cards

PRICING & BUDGET POLICY:
- All prices are inclusive of taxes.
- Minimum drink price is ₹100 (Green Tea).
- Maximum drink price is ₹250 (Chocolate Frappe).
- When a customer specifies a budget (e.g. ₹200), only items with price_inr <= budget may be recommended.

CUSTOMER SERVICE RULES:
- If a customer requests a product not present in this knowledge base (e.g., "strawberry protein smoothie"), state politely that the item is unavailable and not on our menu.
- Always provide accurate pricing in INR.
